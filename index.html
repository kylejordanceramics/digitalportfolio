My work focuses on functional pottery made for everyday use. As a ceramic artist, I find inspiration in everyday objects and the things I see around me. Before making a piece, I think about its shape, purpose, and how I can experiment with glazes and textures. My process involves firing my work in atmospheric kilns, such as wood and soda kilns, which result in unpredictable surfaces. I enjoy how each firing creates different colors, patterns, and textures, making each ceramic piece a symbol of the time-spent, labor, and community involved in making it. This site shows my exploration in Digital Media Art as a ceramic artist.
