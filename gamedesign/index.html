The idea for this game is that every time you move your cursor over the bug, you catch one, and another one moves to a different location on the screen. Each time you catch a bug, you earn one point. The game has three levels, with five points needed to complete each level. You need a total of 15 points to win. The main idea of the game is to help me avoid getting bug bites by catching all 15 bugs.
