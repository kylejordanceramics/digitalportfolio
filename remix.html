For this project, I combined three images: Squidward in his bed, Plankton holding a gun outside, and a scroll. The original Squidward image comes from an episode where he tells Spongebob a joke that makes him laugh all day, so he can’t fall asleep. I remixed the images to create a new story where Squidward has a secret ceramic glaze formula that Plankton wants to steal. The scroll represents the glaze recipe instead of the Krabby Patty formula. Squidward looks tired and stressed because he is trying to keep the recipe safe, while Plankton holding a gun makes him seem determined to get it. Glaze formulas are either openly shared or kept private depending on the artist, so I wanted to poke fun at that. These images work together to create a funny but slightly threatening situation that connects with the characters’ personalities with debates about intellectual property in ceramics.
